Why does a monthly report slow down checkout, and why do analytics databases store data sideways? This explainer walks through the second half of Chapter 3 of Designing Data-Intensive Applications with animated diagrams: OLTP vs. OLAP, data warehouses and ETL, star and snowflake schemas, column-oriented storage, bitmap compression and vectorized processing, sort orders, LSM-style writes, and materialized views and data cubes.

Questions to ask about your own system
• Where do your heavy reports run: on the database serving your users, or on a copy built for analysis?
• Which queries scan millions of rows but use only a few columns?
• Which totals do you recompute on every dashboard load that could be computed once?
