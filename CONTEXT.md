# mahdiyar.me

Mahdiyar's personal presence on the web, served at `mahdiyar.me`. It is separate from his work material and from the products hosted on the same domain.

## Language

**Personal Site**:
The site at the root of `mahdiyar.me` that introduces Mahdiyar himself. Until it exists, a Coming-soon Page stands in for it.
_Avoid_: Homepage, portfolio, landing

**Coming-soon Page**:
The placeholder shown at the root of the domain while the Personal Site is not yet built.
_Avoid_: Splash, maintenance page

**Blog**:
Mahdiyar's personal writing in Persian, published as Posts under `mahdiyar.me/blog`. It never contains Work Docs.
_Avoid_: Docs, decks, notes

**Post**:
A single piece of writing in the Blog.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, deck

**Work Docs**:
Work documents and slide decks for Milli, published separately from this site (`mahdiyar-docs`). Not part of the Blog.
_Avoid_: Blog, posts
